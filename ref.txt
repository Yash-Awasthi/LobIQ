- Reuters reports that part of Jane Street's recent gains come from medium-frequency strategies, not only microseconds.

My picks for LobIQ, highest value first

1. Multi-level OFI and cross-impact (2023). An extension of existing research, measured on real Nasdaq days, with lagged cross-asset forecasting.
2. Exporting ITCH days in LOBSTER format. Anyone could then train LOBCAST, LOBFrame or DeepMarket on free real Nasdaq data. Good for both SWE and quant credibility.
3. A market-realism scorecard. Synthetic vs real days on the statistics the TRADES and LOB-Bench papers use: spread, order sizes, arrival gaps, volatility clustering.
4. A gym-style RL environment on the replay exchange.
5. A "how firms work" docs page mapping HRT, Jane Street and Optiver practice (huge pages, FPGA, OCaml) to LobIQ's components.

Tell me which to build. I'd start with 1 and 2.
